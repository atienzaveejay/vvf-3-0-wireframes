VVF 3.0 · Visual directions · Light mode

Three ways to make it vibrant without throwing away the brand.

Same two screens in each: the feed, and why it worked. These are the screens that carry the product, so they are the fairest test of a direction. Pick one and I will build all eight screens plus the landing page in it.

VVF already has a colour. #FF4F5E appears on the live site and again in the 2.0 redesign, alongside warm neutrals (#171312 ink, #FBFAF9 paper) and Space Grotesk. All three directions keep that coral rather than invent a new palette, because it is real equity and it costs nothing to respect. What changes is what gets built around it.

DIRECTION A

Warm Studio

The existing brand, properly designed. Warm paper ground, coral doing all the work, generous spacing. Feels calm and credible, closest to what exists today, lowest risk with Ivan. The trade: it is the least surprising of the three.

DIRECTION C

Chroma

Coral stays the action colour everywhere, but each category owns a hue that tints the surfaces. Browse skincare and the app is pink; switch to fitness and it turns green. Most distinctive of the three, and the colour is doing a job rather than decorating. The trade: more to build, and it needs discipline to stop it getting noisy.

How to choose

Pick on energy, not on detail. A if you want Ivan to see continuity with what exists. B if you want it to look like a serious tool people pay for. C if you want it to feel unlike anything else in the category. Photography is a placeholder in all three and would be the single biggest upgrade to any of them.
