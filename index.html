01

What changed in this revision

Sign in moved earlier

It now sits in onboarding, paid for by an analysis of their own account rather than asked for cold.

The wall moved

It was on capability. It is now on volume: more videos and more categories, which is what we charge for.

The plan is gone

A six-step shot list is homework. The adaptation survives as one stealable line they can copy in a second.

Category picker keeps free text

Pick a category or name your own, so nobody bounces because their niche is missing.

Filming question split in two

What you make and what you film with are different questions and were answering over each other.

Free tier trains the paid habit

Swipe videos, then get prompted to search more categories. Both are the things the paid tier sells.

04

Design rules

The handle is bought, not asked for

Screen 03 only works because screen 04 pays for it immediately. If the analysis is weak, the sign in becomes a naked gate.

No homework

Nothing we hand a creator should take more than a few seconds to act on. One line they can copy beats a shot list they have to follow.

Free tier is a rehearsal

Swiping videos and searching categories are exactly what the paid tier sells. Free is practice, not a sample.

Count down out loud

"3 of 5 free" on every screen. The paywall should never be the first time they learn there is a limit.

Mobile web, TikTok grammar

No install step. Full-bleed vertical cards, actions on a right rail. Nothing new to learn.

Self-contained

No external fonts or scripts. The page renders on a slow or filtered connection.

05

Still open

Free tier numbers

5 videos and 2 categories are placeholders. Worth testing 3 against 10, since this single number sets the whole conversion rate.

Price

Unset. Small creators are the lowest-paying segment there is, so the number matters more here than in most products.

Handle without TikTok auth

If someone types a handle instead of connecting, how much can we actually analyze? That decides whether screen 04 lands.

Search latency

Still the one hard blocker. If a new category takes minutes, screen 07 breaks the habit we just built.
