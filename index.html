03

Design rules

Mobile web, not an app

No install step between a link and the product. The drawer becomes a left rail on desktop.

TikTok grammar

Full-bleed vertical cards, metadata over the image, actions on a right rail. Nothing new to learn.

Value before account

Two questions and a working feed with no email. The wall sits at step 05, never earlier.

Under 30 seconds to first recommendation

Served from a precomputed catalog. Only the plan adaptation runs live.

Three reasons, not seven tabs

Depth is friction for someone who wants to film something today.

Only shootable videos

If a recommendation needs a crew, it should never have been recommended.

Draft wireframes, not final visual design. Greyboxes stand in for video; copy is written at final intent so it can be reviewed as a product decision.
Section markers use film production revision-stock colors, the convention for marking which document you are holding.
