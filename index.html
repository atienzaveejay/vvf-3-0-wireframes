02

Every note, and what happened to it

LandingOptimise for mobile. Mobile traffic goes to mobile.
Done, and it found a real bug. The hero had zero horizontal padding because a later rule overrode it, which nobody could see on a desktop screen. Beyond that: a full-width primary button, a single upright card instead of a fanned stack of three, a trimmed nav, a sticky action bar, 44px tap targets and two breakpoints under 640px.
Design styleLike 3. Also like the highlight from 2. Try a light blue TikTok highlight or other accents.
Four accents, same screen four times. Chroma keeps the room, direction B lends it the flat highlight block. Tried in TikTok cyan, a softer sky blue, B’s original lime, and the category’s own hue. Cyan is the recommendation, and the reasoning is on the page: VVF’s coral is already within a few degrees of TikTok’s red, so the cyan is the half of that palette we do not have yet.
P1 · homeBig main search box. Lots of suggestions underneath. Tap a suggestion to search.
Screen 01. The six-tile category grid is gone. The suggestions underneath are the grid now, in three groups: niches, products people are searching, and three example queries. Every one of them is a one-tap search.
P2 · Q2 and Q3What’s your main goal? What’s your biggest problem?
Screen 02, both questions, all ten options. The old questions about what they film and what they film with are gone. Both new answers get used: the goal reranks the feed, the problem picks the third card on screen 04.
03 · connectHandle plus email or Gmail to start. TikTok connect optional later. Never post.
Screen 03 has no TikTok button on it. Handle, then Google or email. “We only read what’s already public, and we never post anything, ever” sits under the fold line. The connect reappears at the bottom of screen 04 instead, which is a better moment to ask.
03 · teaseKeep the analysis tease. Make it feel special.
The tease is now a live card, not a promise. It shows the analysis already running against their handle, with items ticking off. The email is the price of seeing a result that is visibly already half-finished, rather than the price of getting in.
04 · payoffLock what AI can retrieve. List the impactful data before we design the screen.
This got its own page. Twenty signals across three sources, each with where it comes from, what it costs and whether it changes what they film. Screen 04 is then built from the six in Tier 1 and nothing else. The short version: the single most explanatory number in short form is retention, it cannot be scraped, and that is the argument for keeping the connect on the table.
05 · copyBased on your profile, here are top breakout videos for inspo.
In, near enough verbatim. “Based on your profile, here are the videos breaking out in skincare right now.”
05 · feedDo not pre-analyse every video. Light cards. Full analysis after they tap analyse. Scroll for more.
Screen 05 is a list now, not one full-bleed card. Each card is a thumbnail, the breakout multiple, who made it and an Analyse button. No findings until something is tapped, which is what makes showing eight of them cheap and makes tapping one feel like spending something.
06 · analyseWhy it worked. Try this. Creative brief, send to self. Share. Remove next video, go back to results.
Screen 06, all four blocks, in that order. Next video is gone and the top-left is now “Back to results”. The brief is three lines rather than a shot list, since a shot list was the thing that got cut last round, and it emails to themselves.
Caps8 videos, 3 searches, 5 analyses. Lean trial on the analyses.
Screens 07, 08 and 09, one for each. They ask for different things on purpose: the video cap offers a sideways move first, the search cap is the hard one because search is the product, and the analysis cap offers the trial. Details are on screen 07’s note.
ThroughoutMobile-first. Breakout, not outlier.
Both applied everywhere. Outlier no longer appears in any user-facing copy on any page. Every screen is drawn at a real 306×648 phone viewport and nothing overflows it, which is why a couple of the cards got shorter.
03

Three things worth a second look

The 8-video cap fights “scroll for more”

An endless feed that stops dead at eight is a small broken promise. It works if the cap arrives as the ninth card in the list rather than a screen that takes over. Screen 07 is drawn as a takeover for now because it is easier to review that way.

Three caps is three numbers to tune

Each one needs its own answer and each one is a chance to interrupt somebody who was enjoying themselves. Worth agreeing which single cap we actually expect most people to hit first, because that is the one the pricing conversation should be built around.

Screen 04 has no fallback yet

A private account, or one with fewer than five public videos, leaves that screen with nothing to say. At an audience of creators under 10k that is not an edge case, and it needs a designed answer rather than an error.

04

Still open

The accent

Cyan is a recommendation, not a decision. It is one variable and every screen follows it, so switching to sky, lime or the category hue costs nothing right now and costs a lot later.

Cost per signup

Reading someone’s videos happens before they have paid anything. Whether we run it for every signup or only on request changes what screen 03 is allowed to promise.

The three cap numbers

8, 3 and 5 are placeholders taken straight from the note. They are also the whole conversion rate.

Price and trial length

Both unset. Screen 09 says “no card for the first week”, which is a decision waiting to happen rather than one already made.
